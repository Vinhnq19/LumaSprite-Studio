# Animation from a stable design

Start from the app-owned helper pixels and resolved source frame. Include the exact named helper; use its exported asset as base or the requested slot stamp. Never replace helper content or redraw an approved character from memory. Preserve face/hood shape, equipment side, palette ramps, body mass, canvas, anchor, duration, loop and frozen slots. Reuse stable construction for moving parts instead of independently redesigning each frame.

Every explicit pxl frame starts from shared items/base, not from the previous frame. Erase the OLD location of moving pixels before drawing their NEW location so old limbs do not remain as ghosts. Follow the supplied per-slot clear/stamp contract when present. Avoid shifting the entire character to imitate limb motion.

Locked-region work is a local edit: use the permitted mask and keep all other RGBA exact. A blink changes eyelids/eye pixels, not head size or face lighting. For four requested blink frames, use open, half closed, closed, open; keep pupils/eyebrows unchanged unless needed by the motion. Do not add a walk-style bob to a blink.

For a four-frame walk, use contact, passing, opposite contact, passing. Distinguish foot placement and knee articulation; a whole-body translation is not a walk. Arms oppose the moving legs, while a held prop stays attached to its hand. Keep planted feet on the established ground line, retain limb thickness, and allow only motion-appropriate body compression. A supported foot must not drift arbitrarily. With more requested frames, add down/up transitions rather than duplicating poses to meet the count. Do not increase the requested count.

For an eight-frame walk, establish contact/down/passing/up for each side before placing pixels. Make a small pose table of hip, knee, ankle and wrist coordinates, then construct each limb as connected tapered masses using the same widths and material ramps. The supporting ankle stays on the source ground line; the swinging foot lifts and changes position. Draw coherent lit/shadow planes, not scattered recolored pixels. Erase the entire old moving limb region, including its old outline, before its replacement; do not stamp or shift a rectangular cutout containing background, torso or the other leg. Keep stable head/torso/equipment pixels from the source whenever the motion permits, and attach hands/props at their actual joints. Check contact-to-passing and final-to-first transitions; arbitrary alternating leg patches are not a completed walk cycle.

A run may have a flight phase; an idle or blink usually does not. Choose secondary cloth/hair motion only when the request and mask allow it. Never apply fixed bob distances, stride lengths or heroic poses to every subject.

Respect exact configured durations even when another rhythm might look better. Keep the last-to-first transition intentional without forcing identical frames for every loop. Before returning the single JSON proposal, check that pose order, all explicit frames and attached equipment remain consistent. No extra AI critique pass.
